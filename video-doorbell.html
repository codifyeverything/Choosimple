04 Misleading signals

What sounds convincing — but isn’t

Resolution claims

Higher resolution does not automatically mean better ownership. Field of view, alert quality, night video, and app reliability often matter more.

No-subscription claims

No-subscription models can be excellent, but they are not automatically simpler. Local storage, app behavior, and alert reliability still matter.

Smart-home branding

A familiar smart-home brand can help, but the best choice still depends on your household, wiring, Wi-Fi, and whether you want subscriptions.

Battery-life promises

Battery-life claims vary widely because motion traffic, cold weather, recording settings, and Wi-Fi strength can change real-world results.
