04 Misleading signals

What sounds convincing — but isn’t

Maximum temperature

More heat can create more damage, not better ownership. Heat control and consistency matter more.

“One-pass” claims

Real results depend on hair type, section size, plate glide, and whether the tool holds heat consistently.

Viral popularity

A straightener can look impressive online before anyone knows whether the plates, cord, hinge, or controls hold up.

Luxury tech

More technology can mean more cost, more weight, more complexity, and more things to maintain.
