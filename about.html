How decisions are made

Every category goes through the same process:

  • We identify products that consistently appear across expert testing, large buyer datasets, and long-term ownership discussions
  • We filter out models with recurring failure patterns, durability complaints, or design issues that show up over time
  • We apply a reliability-first framework — what actually holds up after months of daily use, not just first-impression reviews
  • We select one clear winner per category: the most defensible purchase decision

Why reliability comes first

Most people don't return their purchases. They live with them. That means durability, ease of use, and long-term owner satisfaction matter far more than peak performance in a controlled test.

Choosimple weights those signals accordingly. A product that works well for two years beats one that performs slightly better but fails at the 10-month mark.
