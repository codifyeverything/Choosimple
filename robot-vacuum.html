04 Misleading signals

What sounds convincing — but isn’t

Higher suction ratings

Raw Pa numbers do not guarantee better carpet pickup. Brush design and pressure matter just as much as airflow.

LiDAR mapping

LiDAR is standard now, not premium. The real difference is obstacle avoidance and re-mapping after furniture moves.

“Self-emptying”

You still empty the dock bag every 6–8 weeks. It is less frequent, not hands-off.

Robot mopping claims

Robot mops are for maintenance cleaning, not sticky spills or proper deep mopping.

06 FAQ

What buyers still want to know

Does it actually mop well?

For daily maintenance on hardwood and tile, yes. For sticky or dried spills, no — you will still need a mop by hand.

How loud is it?

Standard mode is quieter than a dishwasher. Max mode is noticeably louder but still usable during the day.

Will it pick up pet hair on carpet?

Yes, consistently. The tangle-resistant brush is one of the strongest reasons this model beats many older premium vacuums for pet homes.

How often does the dock need maintenance?

Empty the dust bag every 6–8 weeks. Refill water and clean the mop tray weekly if you mop daily.

Is the app necessary?

Strongly recommended for setup, zone cleaning, and schedules. Basic cleaning works without it, but you lose the best automation features.
