04 Misleading signals

What sounds convincing — but isn't

Signal #1

Higher Pa suction ratings don't guarantee better carpet pickup. Brush design and pressure matter just as much as raw airflow.

Signal #2

LiDAR mapping is standard now, not premium. The difference lives in obstacle avoidance and re-mapping after furniture moves.

Signal #3

"Self-emptying" still requires you to empty the dock bag every 6–8 weeks. It's less-frequent, not hands-off.

Signal #4

Mopping robots rarely clean a sticky spill — they spread it. Mop mode is for maintenance, not spot cleanup.

09 FAQ

What buyers still want to know

Does it actually mop well?

For daily maintenance on hardwood and tile, yes. For sticky or dried spills, no — you'll still need a mop by hand. The Qrevo Curv's lifting mop correctly avoids carpet when switching surfaces.

How loud is it?

Standard mode is about 58–62 dB — quieter than a dishwasher. Max mode is noticeably louder but still usable during the workday.

Will it pick up pet hair on carpet?

Yes, consistently. The tangle-resistant brush is one of the strongest reasons this model beats older premium vacuums on pet-friendly households.

How often does the dock need maintenance?

Empty the dust bag every 6–8 weeks. Refill water and clean the mop tray weekly if you're mopping daily. Dock filter swap about every 3–4 months.

Is the app necessary?

Strongly recommended for setup, zone-based cleaning, and schedule control. Basic cleaning works without it, but you lose the best automation features.
