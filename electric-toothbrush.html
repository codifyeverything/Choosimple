04 Misleading signals

What sounds convincing — but isn’t

Premium models clean better

Most premium electric brushes do not clean meaningfully better than strong mid-range ones for the average user.

App-connected coaching

Most owners stop checking the app within a few weeks. It adds friction, not better habits.

UV sanitizers and extras

These features sound advanced but usually do not change real oral-health outcomes.

Replacement-head branding

The biggest long-term cost is often the replacement-head ecosystem, not the handle itself.
