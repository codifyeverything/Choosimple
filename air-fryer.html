04 Misleading signals

What sounds convincing — but isn't

Signal #1

More presets don't equal better cooking. Most preset programs are identical time/temp combinations dressed up with different labels.

Signal #2

Smart app connectivity rarely gets used after the first week. Owners overwhelmingly reach for physical dials and buttons.

Signal #3

Dual-basket designs double your capacity in theory. In practice, each basket is smaller than a single 6-quart and harder to clean.

Signal #4

A "dishwasher safe" basket survives far fewer cycles than owners expect. Handwashing extends the coating's life by years.

09 FAQ

What buyers still want to know

Is the 6-quart large enough for a family of four?

Yes, for most meals. It handles a whole chicken, 2 lb of wings, or four chicken breasts. For a family of 5+ cooking side dishes simultaneously, a dual-zone model may serve better.

Does the ClearCook window really help?

It's genuinely useful. You can monitor browning without opening the basket and losing heat — a small quality-of-life upgrade that matters daily.

How long does the basket coating last?

With plastic or silicone utensils and handwashing, owners consistently report 3+ years of use. Metal utensils and dishwashing cycles cut that to under 12 months.

Can I use aluminum foil or parchment?

Yes, but don't block the airflow vents. Perforated parchment designed for air fryers is the cleanest solution for sticky or saucy foods.

Does it replace an oven?

For most weekday cooking — yes. For baking, large roasts, or anything needing more than 6-quart capacity, you'll still reach for the oven.
